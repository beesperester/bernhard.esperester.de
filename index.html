    how to play

    You start out a kitten: bugs, butterflies and the food lying about are all you can manage. At 100 snacks you're a young cat with a name of your own, jumping up onto rocks and into the trees after mice, frogs, fish and birds. At 500 you're fully grown: pounce, lure fish with a splash, shake the trees, catch birds and bats on the wing.

    Your cat lives in this browser. To take it to another one, copy its collar tag and put it on there. Keep the tag to yourself: whoever has it is your cat.
